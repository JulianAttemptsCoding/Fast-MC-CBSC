1. EXECUTIVE AUDIT OPINION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The CBSC-ZDC specification is a serious, auditor-ready design document. Its main
structural ideas are sound:

  - Rejecting monotone per-layer deposits is physically correct.
  - Enforcing monotone remaining budget is mathematically clean.
  - The exact anti-dust decoder is provably correct under feasibility conditions.
  - The staged factorization addresses known prior failure modes.
  - The evaluation plan is broad and appropriate for HEP fast simulation.
  - The separation between structural validity and empirical fidelity is correct.

However, the specification is not yet fully implementation-ready because several
data-contract and probabilistic-semantics issues remain open:

  - The ROOT/data target has not been numerically audited.
  - The definition of total response T, reserve Rres, subthreshold U_l, active
    layer A_l, and visible V must be frozen precisely.
  - The shared latent z needs a clear probabilistic role.
  - Count feasibility must be enforced dynamically at sampling.
  - Hard Top-k requires an explicit training-time support-learning path.
  - Performance and evaluation acceptance criteria must be predeclared.

Overall disposition:
  DESIGN STATUS: Conditional pass.
  IMPLEMENTATION STATUS: Not approved until critical/high findings resolved.
  PHYSICS FIDELITY STATUS: Not evaluated; cannot be claimed from this audit.

6. MATHEMATICAL AUDIT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.1 Proposition 5.1: Monotone remaining budget
  STATUS: PASS.
  PROOF RECHECK:
    R_l = T - sum_{j=0}^l D_j.
    R_{l+1} = R_l - D_{l+1}.
    If D_{l+1} >= 0, then R_{l+1} <= R_l.
    Also R_l = Rres + sum_{j>l} D_j >= Rres >= 0.
    No constraint D_{l+1} <= D_l is implied.

6.2 Theorem 8.1: No-dust exact-count exact-budget decoder
  STATUS: PASS UNDER FEASIBILITY.
  CONDITIONS:
    B > 0, 1 <= K <= N_l, B >= K tau, |S_l|=K, w_i>0, sum w_i=1.
  PROOF RECHECK:
    Unselected: Y_i=0.
    Selected: Y_i=tau+(B-K tau)w_i >= tau.
    Sum: K tau + (B-K tau)*1 = B.
    Positive count: exactly K.
    No 0<Y<tau: selected >= tau, unselected = 0.

6.3 Corollary 8.2: Event accounting
  STATUS: PASS UNDER LAYER ACCOUNTING.
  PROOF RECHECK:
    For each layer, either resolved sum_i Y_i = D_l or unresolved U_l = D_l.
    Sum_l D_l + Rres = T.
    Therefore sum_i Y_i + sum_l U_l + Rres = T.

6.4 Factorization notation
  STATUS: ISSUE.
  NOTES: S is used for first visible layer and selected cell support.
  RECOMMENDATION: Rename first visible layer to S_vis or L0; rename cell support
  to C_l or S_cell,l.

6.5 Latent z semantics
  STATUS: ISSUE.
  NOTES: z ~ N(0,I) is introduced, but no prior regularization, encoder, or
  marginal likelihood is specified. It may be a stochastic conditioning input
  rather than a latent variable in a probabilistic density model.
  RECOMMENDATION: Explicitly define z as one of:
    Option A: Pure noise conditioning variable, not claimed to induce latent
              event structure.
    Option B: VAE/flow latent with q(z | truth) and prior p(z), with ELBO or
              equivalent objective.
    Option C: Conditional latent sampled from a learned prior p(z | c) with
              explicit training protocol.

16. RESEARCH IDEAS AND OPTIONAL IMPROVEMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
These are not required upgrades. They are ablations or future options.

R-01 Conditional latent z.
  Replace pure noise z with amortized latent q(z | truth) or conditional prior
  p(z | c) if event-level latent structure is desired.

R-02 Endpoint atoms for rho.
  Add explicit point mass at rho=1 or near-1 component if data show endpoint.

R-03 Zero-inflated layer profile.
  Use layer-level hurdle or zero-inflated logistic-normal to handle exact zero
  layers more explicitly than active Bernoulli plus simplex.

R-04 Count model alternatives.
  Test grouped categorical, Poisson-binomial, or hierarchical count models if
  per-layer N_l is large or count distributions are highly structured.

R-05 Support relaxation alternatives.
  Compare Gumbel-Top-k with sparsemax, entmax, hard-concrete, continuous subset
  relaxation.

R-06 Scheduled sampling.
  Gradually replace truth counts/support/profile with generated ones to reduce
  exposure bias.

R-07 Latent graph compression.
  If full-node CFM is too slow, learn a geometry-aware latent representation and
  decode to cells, but only after a faithful full-state teacher exists.

R-08 Mixture-of-experts routing.
  Route by total response regime, first visible layer, ECAL fraction, or late
  energy to handle heterogeneous ZDC response.

R-09 Point-cloud decoder.
  If threshold and readout mapping are frozen, compare against point-cloud
  generation for sparse hit sets.

R-10 Distillation.
  Distill ODE solver to fewer steps only after fidelity teacher is established.

R-11 Tail-aware objectives.
  Add quantile or tail diagnostics, but avoid turning generation into eventwise
  regression.

R-12 Reconstruction-aware diagnostics.
  Use frozen reconstruction as evaluation, not as a hidden training target that
  overfits downstream metric.
