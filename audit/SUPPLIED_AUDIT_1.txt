[T00] SCOPE DECISION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Thought: The document explicitly disclaims fidelity ("research design, not a
fidelity claim"). So the audit must NOT be graded on "does it reproduce Geant4."
It must be graded on the three things it *does* assert:
  (a) the stated invariants are actually implied by the stated construction;
  (b) the cited evidence supports the claims mapped to it (Appendix A);
  (c) the specified training procedure can actually learn the specified model.
(c) is the one an auditor is most likely to skip, because it is stated in prose
across Sec 4 and Sec 9 rather than in a theorem. Prioritise it.

Idea: the document is unusually self-critical, which creates a specific failure
mode -- a reader may treat the extensive caveating as evidence of rigour and stop
checking. Audit assumption: every proved statement is true and every *unproved*
statement is suspect until re-derived. That split turned out to be exactly right
(all four theorems/propositions hold; four blocking defects are all in unproved
prose).

[T05] DATA-CONTRACT QA -- KINETIC vs TOTAL ENERGY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Thought while reading eq (4)-(5) and Sec 3.5: the spec validates
E^2 - p^2 = m_n^2 and then defines rho = T/E in [0,1]. Both statements assume E
is TOTAL energy. But Geant4's primary generator does not use total energy.

Source: G4ParticleGun.hh (multiple mirrors: apc.u-paris.fr, hurel.hanyang.ac.kr,
        ecce-eic.github.io) and CERN Indico "Detector Simulation / Primary
        particles" (Pokorski & Ribon).
  Confirmed: SetParticleEnergy(G4double aKineticEnergy); the UI command
  /gun/energy is documented as "Set kinetic energy". SetParticleMomentum() sets
  direction and "particle_energy(Kinetic Energy)".
  => If the ROOT branch stores the gun energy, it is KINETIC energy, and eq (4)
  fails identically by construction (it would need E_tot = T_kin + m_n).

Arithmetic: m_n = 939.565 MeV.
  at T_kin =  50 GeV : E_tot/T_kin - 1 = 1.879 %
  at T_kin = 250 GeV : E_tot/T_kin - 1 = 0.376 %
Thought: a 0.4-1.9 % ambiguity in the denominator of rho is the same order as the
energy-scale closure a calorimeter paper is trying to demonstrate. So this is not
pedantry -- it silently biases the headline observable across the reporting range,
and it biases it *energy-dependently*, which will look like a non-linearity.
DEFECT M2 recorded. Sec 10.1 item 1 ("units") must be sharpened to "kinetic vs
total energy convention", and eq (4)/(5) must state which E is meant.

Second thought on the same section: Sec 3.5 asks whether "legitimate T > E events"
occur. For neutrons, invisible energy (nuclear binding, neutrinos, escaping
neutrons) normally makes T well below T_kin -- so rho << 1 and bounded support is
safe at 50-250 GeV. BUT the R2 training-range experiment (Sec 10.4) extends down
toward 0 GeV, where radiative capture (n,gamma) releases the neutron separation
energy, of order 6-8 MeV, which can exceed the incident kinetic energy of a slow
neutron. So T > E_kin is not merely a hypothetical accounting artefact -- there is
a concrete physical mechanism for it at the low end of the R2 support. Sec 3.5 is
right to make the bound conditional; the audit should name the mechanism so the
data auditor knows what to look for and does not dismiss low-energy overflow as a
bug.
IDEA E-phys recorded.

Third thought: Sec 3.5 says rho = T/E in [0,1], but does not say what value rho
actually takes. If Y is raw Geant4 deposit in the *active* material of a sampling
calorimeter, rho is set by the sampling fraction and will sit at a few percent,
not near 1. A 4-component Beta mixture on [0,1] then has to represent a narrow
spike near the left edge, requiring extreme alpha/beta and giving bad
conditioning. Recommend modelling a standardised rho' = rho / rho_bar(E), or a
logit-normal on rho, and reporting the empirical rho support in the Stage 0
data-contract report. Not a defect in the algebra, a defect in the parameterisation.
IDEA E-rho recorded.

[T08] BLOCKING DEFECT B1 -- THE SHARED LATENT z CANNOT BE LEARNED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Setup: eq (12) factorises the joint with a shared event latent z ~ N(0,I) entering
EVERY factor. Sec 4 states its purpose: "to preserve event-level dependence among
response, start depth, occupancy, and morphology rather than sampling all stages
independently." That is the entire justification for z's existence.

Now read Sec 9. Every head is trained by a conditional likelihood or a regression:
  L_V   = BCE(V, pi_V)                        eq (42)
  L_rho = -log p(rho | V=1, c, z)             eq (43)
  L_S   = -log p(S  | V=1, c, z, T)           eq (44)
  L_A   = BCE(A, pi_A)                        eq (45)
  L_K   = sum_l CE(K_l, eta_l)                eq (46)
  L_FM  = E|| v_theta(X_t,t,c,z,G,D,K) - u_t ||^2_W    eq (33)
and z is drawn from the prior, INDEPENDENTLY of the data, at training time. The
document never specifies any other procedure -- there is no encoder q(z | event),
no KL term, no ELBO, no adversarial or score-based term in eq (41).

Proof that z is then ignored at the optimum:
  For any head, the objective is E_{x ~ p_data(.|c)} E_{z ~ N(0,I)} [-log p_th(x|c,z)]
  = E_z [ KL( p_data(.|c) || p_th(.|c,z) ) ] + const.
  KL >= 0 with equality iff p_th(.|c,z) = p_data(.|c). Since the outer expectation
  is over z drawn independently of x, the global minimiser sets
  p_th(.|c,z) = p_data(.|c) for almost every z -- i.e. it MARGINALISES z AWAY.
  The same argument applies to L_FM: the regression target u_t = X_1 - X_0 does
  not depend on z, so the optimal velocity field averages over z.

Numerical illustration (CHECK 7), family N(a + b z, s^2), x indep of z, 2e5 draws:
    b = 0.00 -> E[-log p] = 0.4979   <-- minimum
    b = 0.25 -> 0.5288
    b = 0.50 -> 0.6222
    b = 1.00 -> 0.9967
  Analytic: E[(x - a - bz)^2] = Var(x) + (E[x] - a)^2 + b^2, minimised at b = 0.

CONSEQUENCE: the mechanism the factorization advertises does not exist under the
specified training. At convergence each stage samples from its own conditional
marginal, which is exactly the "sampling all stages independently" behaviour
Sec 4 says z is there to prevent. In practice you would not even see this fail
loudly -- every component loss would look healthy, per-observable 1-D histograms
would look fine, and only the JOINT diagnostics of Sec 13.2 item 6 (correlations
among response, start depth, hit count, width, ECAL fraction, late energy) would
be wrong. That is a slow, expensive failure discovered at Stage 6.

Re-QA (is there a reading under which this is fine?):
  - Could the chain itself carry the correlation without z? YES, partially: eq (12)
    already conditions D on (T,S,A), K on D, support on K. That is a genuine
    autoregressive dependence and it is trained correctly. So the cascade is not
    broken -- it just does not need, and does not get, z.
  - Could z be intended as a *sampling-time only* diversity knob? If so, the
    factorization must not claim it preserves event-level dependence, and the
    conditioning of every head on z is misleading.
  - Is there any objective in eq (41) under which z is identified? No.
CONFIRMED as blocking.

FIXES (any one, must be chosen before Stage 1):
  F1. Drop z entirely. Rely on the existing autoregressive conditioning. Cheapest,
      loses nothing that is currently being learned, and makes the factorization
      honest. RECOMMENDED as the default.
  F2. Keep z and add an inference network q_phi(z | event, c) with a KL term,
      i.e. turn the cascade into a hierarchical VAE. Principled, but adds
      posterior-collapse risk and a new tuning surface.
  F3. Keep z and train the whole cascade under a distributional objective
      (MMD / adversarial / score-based on joint summary statistics) in which z is
      identified. Most expensive.
  Whichever is chosen, add a POSITIVE CONTROL to Sec 13: fix c, draw many z, and
  test whether the joint (T, S, K_tot, width) distribution differs from the
  z-marginalised product of marginals. If it does not, z is dead. This test is
  cheap and would have caught the defect at Stage 2.

[T09] BLOCKING DEFECT B2 -- THE RESERVE IS AN UNSUPERVISED ENERGY SINK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
eq (20)-(22): q_res is always a component of the softmax, so w_res > 0 strictly.
Therefore sum_l D_l = T(1 - w_res) < T, ALWAYS and by construction.

But T is defined in eq (16) as T = V*E*rho, with rho fitted (eq 14) to the truth
total response. So the total response of a DECODED event is not T; it is
T(1 - w_res) minus any subthreshold residual. The response model and the decoder
disagree about what "total response" means.

Now check eq (41) for a term that pins w_res: L_V, L_rho, L_S, L_A, L_D, L_K,
L_FM, L_sup, L_aux. L_D is described only as "a masked-simplex likelihood, profile
flow-matching loss, or a set of conditional distributional terms" -- and Remark 5.2
says the reserve's interpretation "must be frozen" but never says what it is
supervised AGAINST. There is no truth quantity for w_res anywhere in Sec 10.2's
canonical tensor list.

Numerical scale (CHECK 6), 40 active layers:
    q_res = -2 -> w_res = 2.27e-3 -> 0.227 % total-response deficit
    q_res = -4 -> w_res = 2.38e-4 -> 0.024 %
    q_res = -6 -> w_res = 3.53e-5 -> 0.004 %
Thought: the magnitude is set entirely by an unconstrained logit. It could be
0.02 % or 5 %. For a calorimeter energy-scale claim, an uncontrolled few-tenths of
a percent is already at the level people argue about.

Sharper point: Sec 1.3 says HGF-ZDC's documented failure was "a large response
bias." This construction reintroduces a response bias by a different route, and
Corollary 8.2 will certify the event as perfectly conserved while it happens,
because the identity is stated in terms of the model's own R_res rather than any
truth quantity. Verified in CHECK 1: |sum Y + sum U + R_res - T| = 0 exactly,
with a non-zero R_res. The QA gate passes and the physics is still wrong.

FIXES:
  F1. Define T as the truth in-detector scored total, remove q_res from the
      simplex, and put leakage OUTSIDE T. Cleanest.
  F2. Keep the reserve but give it a truth target from the Geant4 scorer (energy
      in excluded/invalid channels, or out-of-acceptance leakage), add an explicit
      L_res term to eq (41), and add reserve to Sec 10.2's canonical tensors and
      to Sec 13.1's QA list.
  Either way, Sec 13.1 must gain a check comparing the DECODED total against the
  SAMPLED T, not just the internal identity.

[T10] BLOCKING DEFECT B3 -- DECODER CANNOT REPRESENT (K_l > 0 AND U_l > 0)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Read eq (11) and Theorem 8.1 side by side.

TRUTH semantics, eq (10)-(11):
  Y_i^(tau) = Y_i * 1{Y_i >= tau};  U_l = sum_{i in l} Y_i * 1{0 < Y_i < tau}.
  K*_l = #{i in l : Y_i >= tau} (eq 40).
  Nothing couples K*_l and U_l. A layer with 5 resolved cells and 90 subthreshold
  cells has K*_l = 5 AND U_l > 0. This is the GENERIC configuration in a granular
  hadronic calorimeter -- the subthreshold tail is where most of the cells are.

MODEL semantics, Sec 8.2 + Theorem 8.1:
  If B = D_l >= tau : K >= 1, ALL of B is distributed to the K resolved cells,
                      and U_l = 0.
  If B < tau        : K = 0, all cells zero, U_l = B.
  => model U_l > 0 IFF K_l = 0. The joint configuration (K_l > 0, U_l > 0) is
     UNREACHABLE.

Consequence quantified (CHECK 3), realistic layer with 5 resolved cells
(12.0, 6.1, 3.4, 1.8, 1.1 MeV) plus a 90-cell subthreshold tail, tau = 1 MeV:
    truth D_l = 0.06518 GeV, K* = 5, U* = 0.04078 GeV
      -> 62.6 % of the layer's raw energy is subthreshold
    truth resolved energy               = 0.02440 GeV
    model resolved energy (correct K=5) = 0.06518 GeV
    -> resolved-cell energies inflated by 167.1 %, model U forced to 0.

So the decoder does not delete the subthreshold energy, and it does not record it
either -- it PUSHES IT INTO THE SURVIVING CELLS. This is the mirror image of the
dust failure the decoder was designed to prevent: instead of spreading a little
energy over many empty cells, it piles a lot of energy onto few occupied cells.
The positive-hit spectrum, the maximum-cell fraction, the top-k fractions and R50
(all listed in Sec 13.2 item 5) will all be biased, in a correlated way.

WHY IT WILL NOT BE CAUGHT: every gate in Sec 13.1 still passes. Corollary 8.2
holds; per-layer resolved energy equals D_l - U_l by construction; there is no
dust; the count is exact. The QA suite validates the model against ITS OWN
bookkeeping rather than against eq (11)'s truth decomposition.

Note on scope: in raw-deposit mode (tau = 0) this defect vanishes, since U == 0
by definition. But raw mode is also the mode where the anti-dust decoder buys the
least (Geant4 raw deposits are exactly zero outside the shower and non-zero inside
it, so occupancy is not really the problem). The defect therefore lands precisely
on the mode where the document's central contribution is supposed to matter.

FIX (preserves every invariant in Theorem 8.1):
  Introduce a per-layer resolved fraction phi_l in [0,1], generated alongside K_l
  and conditioned on (c, D_l, K_l, l). Decode with B_resolved = phi_l * D_l and
  set U_l = (1 - phi_l) * D_l. Theorem 8.1 goes through verbatim with B replaced
  by phi_l*D_l; the feasibility condition becomes phi_l*D_l >= K*tau; Corollary 8.2
  is unchanged. Supervise phi_l against the truth resolved fraction, which is
  directly computable from eq (10)-(11) and already implied by the canonical tensor
  list (cell_energy_raw, cell_energy_tau, subthreshold). Add "generated vs truth
  U_l distribution by layer" to Sec 13.2.

[T11] BLOCKING DEFECT B4 -- eq (25) EXCLUDES A PHYSICALLY ORDINARY TRUTH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
eq (25) as written:
    K_l = 0                                if 0 <= D_l < tau
    K_l in {1, ..., min(N_l, floor(D_l/tau))}   if D_l >= tau
So K_l = 0 is FORBIDDEN whenever D_l >= tau.

Counterexample (CHECK 2): a layer whose raw energy is 10*tau spread over 40 cells
at 0.25*tau each. Truth K* = 0 (no cell clears threshold), truth U* = 10*tau.
    eq (25) allowed counts = 1..10; K* = 0 is EXCLUDED.
    The masked categorical (eq 24) assigns P(K_l = 0) = 0, so
    L_K = -log 0 = +inf.
This is not an exotic edge case. It is the standard "diffuse halo layer" -- a
layer far from the shower core, or the tail of a late shower, where energy is
present but spread thin. Such layers are common at the edges of the longitudinal
profile, exactly the region Sec 13.2 item 3 wants to measure ("late-energy
fraction").

Two separate consequences:
  (a) Training instability: a NaN/inf in L_K on the first such event. Depending on
      the masking implementation this may present as a silent NaN propagating
      through eq (41), which is much worse than a crash.
  (b) Even with the NaN patched away, the model is structurally incapable of
      generating a diffuse sub-threshold layer, so the generated longitudinal
      support will be systematically too "crisp."

Related: eq (25) is also undefined at tau = 0, since floor(D_l / 0) does not
exist. Sec 6 handles raw-deposit mode in prose immediately afterwards, but the
equation itself is a landmine for anyone implementing from the contract.

FIX: allow K_l = 0 for every D_l, i.e. support {0, 1, ..., min(N_l,
floor(D_l/tau))}, and route the whole budget to U_l when K_l = 0. This composes
correctly with the phi_l fix from T10 (K_l = 0 is just phi_l = 0). Write eq (25)
with an explicit tau > 0 guard and a separate raw-mode line.

[T12] MAJOR -- CAUSAL LAYER MASK IS UNJUSTIFIED IN A PARALLEL FIELD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Sec 7.4 step 5 imposes a causal layer mask so layer l attends only to 0..l, and
Sec 3.2 requires longitudinal graph edges to point shallow->deep, with a
bidirectional graph relegated to "an ablation."

Thought: causal masking earns its keep in an AUTOREGRESSIVE model, where it
implements a factorisation p(x) = prod_l p(x_l | x_<l) and is required for the
likelihood to be valid. This model is NOT autoregressive over layers. It is a
single velocity field integrated in flow time t, updating all nodes simultaneously
(Sec 7.1: "The proposed field updates all nodes at every numerical integration
step"). There is no layer-index factorisation to protect. The mask therefore does
not enforce any probabilistic property -- it only removes capacity.

Worse, the removed capacity is capacity you want. Conditioned on (T, D, K), the
correlations between layers are SYMMETRIC: knowing that layer 50 is unusually
active is informative about layer 20 (it says the shower started late), and the
model is explicitly conditioned on the whole profile D anyway, so no causal
information barrier is even being maintained. The mask blocks a path the
conditioning has already opened.

Also note the mask operates on POOLED layer tokens (step 4), so it constrains only
aggregate longitudinal information; cell-level longitudinal structure travels
exclusively over the directed graph edges. Which leads to the next point.

RECOMMENDATION: make bidirectional layer attention and bidirectional longitudinal
edges the DEFAULT, and demote the causal variant to the ablation. If causality is
retained for physical-intuition reasons, the spec must state what probabilistic
property it enforces -- and I do not believe there is one.

[T13] MAJOR -- THE STATED ADVANTAGE OVER LAYER-AUTOREGRESSION IS OVERSTATED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Sec 7.1 rejects a 65-call rollout on three grounds: (i) invalid index
correspondence across irregular layers, (ii) accumulated free-running error,
(iii) serial latency.

(ii) and (iii) are legitimate and well argued. (i) is not. The parallel model
carries cell-level longitudinal dependence ONLY through the directed longitudinal
graph edges, and Sec 3.2 requires those edges to be built "from audited physical
adjacency or overlap." Constructing a physically valid shallow->deep edge set
across irregular layers is the SAME problem as constructing a valid cross-layer
slot correspondence for an autoregressive model. The architecture does not
dissolve the difficulty; it relocates it into the geometry builder.

This matters because Sec 1.3 lists "invalid cross-layer slot correspondence" as a
documented failure of the legacy HurdleGraph model, and Sec 14 lists "Invalid
graph / readout-index edges" as a failure mode. So the project already knows this
is its historical weak point -- and Sec 7.1 implies the new architecture is immune
to it when it is not.

RECOMMENDATION: reword Sec 7.1 to claim (ii) and (iii) only, and elevate the
geometry builder to a Stage 0 deliverable with its own acceptance gate (degree
distribution, isolated-node census, edge-length distribution, and a visual check
that longitudinal edges connect physically overlapping cells).

[T14] MAJOR -- GUMBEL-TOP-k DOUBLE-COUNTS STOCHASTICITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
eq (35) draws S_l = TopK(a_i + g_i) with g_i ~ Gumbel(0,1), citing Kool et al.
[30] (verified: 1903.06059, correct). The Gumbel-Top-k trick samples a size-K
subset without replacement from a categorical distribution whose LOGITS are a_i.

But where do a_i come from? From the flow's support channel s at t = 1. And s at
t = 1 is already a SAMPLE from the generative model -- the randomness has already
been injected through X_0 ~ N(0,I) and integrated along the ODE. The supervised
target for that channel (eq 27) is a near-deterministic two-point value
+/- logit(eps), i.e. the field is trained to output a realised binary mask, not a
distribution's logits.

So the pipeline draws once (the flow), then draws again (Gumbel) from a quantity
that is not a logit. The two stochastic layers are composed without a stated joint
semantics, and the result is over-dispersion: occupancy patterns will be more
spatially random than truth, which will show up as inflated transverse RMS/R90 and
degraded geometry-aware C2ST -- and will be easy to misdiagnose as a graph or
conditioning problem.

Note this is also inconsistent with eq (27)'s own encoding: if s were meant to be
logits, its target would not be a two-point function of the binary mask M_i.

RECOMMENDATION: make DETERMINISTIC Top-K over the flow's support channel the
primary decoder (the flow already carries the event randomness), and treat
Gumbel-Top-k with an explicit temperature as an ablation. If Gumbel is kept as
primary, retrain the support channel to produce calibrated logits (e.g. supervise
against a soft target or a ranking loss) and state the resulting sampling
semantics explicitly.

[T15] MAJOR -- eq (28) TARGET IS UNBOUNDED FOR NEAR-THRESHOLD CELLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
eq (28): r*_i = M_i * [ log(Y_i - tau + eps) - mean_j log(Y_j - tau + eps) ].
As Y_i -> tau+, log(Y_i - tau + eps) -> log(eps), so r*_i -> large negative.

Measured spread (CHECK 5), 5-cell layer with one cell at tau + 1e-9:
    eps = 1e-3  -> r* in [ -1.22,  1.26], spread  2.48
    eps = 1e-6  -> r* in [ -6.46,  2.84], spread  9.30
    eps = 1e-12 -> r* in [-11.99,  4.22], spread 16.21
L_FM (eq 33) is a SQUARED-error velocity loss. A single cell sitting just above
threshold therefore contributes O(r*^2) ~ 10^2 while typical cells contribute
O(1). The loss becomes dominated by the least physically important cells --
exactly the ones whose energies are nearly determined by the threshold floor
anyway.

Tension: eps also controls the round-trip accuracy (see T16), so it cannot simply
be raised. The spec's own instruction that "the decoder and target transform must
be versioned together" is right but insufficient -- it needs a stated criterion.

RECOMMENDATION: (a) set eps to a physically meaningful scale (e.g. a fixed
fraction of the MIP energy in the active medium) rather than a numerical epsilon;
(b) winsorise r* at a stated quantile and report the clipped fraction;
(c) report an eps sensitivity scan as a Stage 4 deliverable; (d) use the weight
matrix W in eq (33), which the spec already allows, to down-weight the share
channel for near-threshold cells.

[T16] POSITIVE FINDING -- THE ENCODE/DECODE PAIR IS EXACTLY SELF-INVERSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Checked whether eq (28)'s centred-log-share encoding actually inverts through
eq (36) softmax + eq (37) decode. It does, and the document never claims it.

Algebra: softmax(r*)_i = (Y_i - tau + eps) / sum_j (Y_j - tau + eps).
Decoding Yhat_i = tau + (B - K tau) * w_i with B = sum_j Y_j recovers Y_i exactly
as eps -> 0, because sum_j (Y_j - tau) = B - K tau.
Numerical (CHECK 4), 5-cell layer, eps = 1e-12: max |Y - Yhat| = 1.8e-12.

VERDICT: CONFIRMED CORRECT, and it is a real design virtue -- the target transform
and the decoder are a matched pair, so a perfectly-trained share channel decodes
to exactly the truth. This should be stated as a lemma in Sec 8 (it is the
sharpest available answer to "is the encoding lossy?") and it also makes the
eps-tail tension of T15 explicit and honest rather than hidden.

[T17] MODERATE -- NOTATION COLLISION INSIDE eq (12)

Sec 4 defines S = first visible layer (a scalar) AND S_l = the selected cell set

[T20] MODERATE -- THE SURVIVAL MODEL IS RENORMALISED INTO NON-IDENTIFIABILITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
eq (17)-(18) define discrete hazards h_l and the product-form p~_l, then the text

the leakage/no-interaction mass live at l = L (which is the honest survival
formulation and pairs naturally with the hurdle V), or keep the renormalisation
and stop calling it a survival model.

[T21] MODERATE -- UNDER-SPECIFIED TRAINING PATHS (SEC 8.4, SEC 9, STAGE 5)

(a) The support channel appears in BOTH L_FM (eq 33, as a CFM regression channel)

[T23] MINOR -- EVALUATION PROTOCOL GAPS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Sec 13 is unusually thorough (classifier two-sample tests, coverage/precision-

extractor). Two things are still missing:
  (a) No statistical power / sample-size calculation. "Report paired/bootstrap

      meaningless without n-seed training replicates, since architecture
      differences here are likely to be smaller than seed variance.
