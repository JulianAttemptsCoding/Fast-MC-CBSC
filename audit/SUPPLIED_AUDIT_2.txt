EXECUTIVE VERDICT
================================================================

VERDICT: NOT YET IMPLEMENTATION-READY AS A FROZEN AUDITOR SPECIFICATION.

The document is substantially stronger than an ordinary architecture proposal. Its main
physics correction is sound; its exact-support decoder theorem is algebraically correct under
its stated assumptions; it clearly separates structural validity from empirical Geant4
fidelity; and the PDF is professionally typeset.

However, six blocking issues prevent the current text from serving as a complete,
internally consistent implementation contract:

B1. Thresholded-readout energy accounting is internally inconsistent.
B2. The shared event latent z is not given a valid marginal likelihood or trainable inference
    mechanism and will generally be ignored under the written losses.
B3. The support CFM and independent Gumbel-Top-k introduce uncalibrated double
    stochasticity and a truth-to-sample mismatch.
B4. The protocol contains no predeclared numerical success, non-inferiority, or failure gates.
B5. The incident-energy convention conflates Geant4 kinetic energy with relativistic total
    energy.
B6. The reserve variable is unobserved, forced positive by softmax, and scientifically
    non-identifiable under the stated target.

The specification should therefore be described as a strong but unfinished research design,
not yet as an exact implementation contract. After correcting B1-B6 and the major items below,
the project would be suitable for implementation as a controlled experimental scaffold.

================================================================
SEVERITY DEFINITIONS
================================================================

BLOCKER
A contradiction, missing mathematical object, or missing experimental rule that can make two
conforming implementations scientifically different, invalidate a claimed invariant, or prevent
the stated model from being trained as described.

MAJOR
A defect likely to bias results, undermine reproducibility, or materially weaken the paper's
scientific claim, but which can be repaired without replacing the full project.

MODERATE
A meaningful ambiguity, incomplete control, or reporting omission that should be fixed before
training or external review.

MINOR
A presentational, citation-format, accessibility, or local clarity problem that does not by itself
invalidate the experiment.

STEP 009 — Raw versus thresholded target-mode audit, pages 6, 9, and 11
Action:
- Traced the definitions of thresholded truth, subthreshold residual U_l, layer budget D_l,
  count feasibility, decoder output, and event accounting.

Result:
- Equation (11) defines U_l as the sum of all raw deposits satisfying 0 < Y_i < tau.
- Equation (25) constrains K_l using D_l/tau.
- Equation (37) allocates the entire layer budget B=D_l to selected cells whenever B>=tau.
- The unresolved branch assigns U_l=B only if the entire layer budget is below tau.
- Therefore, a real thresholded layer that contains both above-threshold and subthreshold raw
  deposits cannot be represented correctly.

Concrete counterexample:
- tau = 1 GeV;
- raw layer cells = (2.0, 0.4, 0, ...);
- D_l interpreted as raw layer energy = 2.4 GeV;
- truth thresholded output is (2.0, 0, ...), and U_l=0.4 GeV.
- With K=1, Equation (37) emits one selected cell with 2.4 GeV and U_l=0.
- The thresholded truth and the raw accounting are both wrong.

QA disposition:
- BLOCKER B1 confirmed.
- Equation (39) is algebraically correct only under the decoder's own U definition, not under
  the truth definition in Equation (11).

STEP 010 — Minimum correction for target modes
Action:
- Constructed mutually consistent alternatives and checked their identities.

Recommended freeze:
MODE A — raw-deposit model
- tau = 0.
- T_raw = sum_i Y_i_raw.
- No subthreshold residual variable is needed.
- Exact support uses strict positivity.

MODE B — thresholded-readout-only model
- Y_i^(tau) = Y_i_raw 1{Y_i_raw >= tau}.
- T_tau = sum_i Y_i^(tau).
- The profile budgets allocate T_tau only.
- No U_l is included in the core target because subthreshold raw energy is not part of the
  modeled readout.

MODE C — joint raw-accounting plus thresholded readout
For every layer:
- B_l_raw >= 0;
- 0 <= U_l <= B_l_raw;
- B_l_plus = B_l_raw - U_l;
- K_l = 0 if B_l_plus=0;
- otherwise 1 <= K_l <= min(N_l, floor(B_l_plus/tau));
- decoded selected energies sum to B_l_plus, not B_l_raw;
- event identity:
    sum_i Yhat_i + sum_l U_l + R_res = T_raw.

QA disposition:
- Any one mode can be made valid.
- The current text mixes MODE B and MODE C and must be rewritten.

STEP 013 — Shared-latent trainability audit
Action:
- Compared the stated role of z with the componentwise BCE/NLL/CFM training losses.

Result:
- If z is sampled independently of each training target and no encoder q_phi(z|Y,c) or
  invertible latent map is provided, the expected proper-scoring-rule optimum is generally to
  ignore z.
- Sampling a common random vector does not by itself preserve dependence between learned
  stage outputs.
- Stagewise teacher-forced conditioning may preserve some dependence without z, but that is
  different from the stated mechanism.

QA disposition:
- BLOCKER B2 confirmed.
- Choose exactly one:
  1. remove z and use explicit ancestral dependence;
  2. add amortized posterior inference and an ELBO/joint latent objective;
  3. use a joint invertible/transport model with a defined latent-to-output map;
  4. train a coupled multi-output model with shared noise whose target coupling is identified.

STEP 016 — Active support and masked simplex audit, pages 8-9
Action:
- Checked Equations (19)-(22), inactive masks, exact zeros, and reserve semantics.

Result:

QA disposition:
- BLOCKER B6 confirmed.

- If an atom at zero reserve is needed, add a reserve hurdle and a supervised target.

STEP 017 — Monotone remaining-budget proposition, page 9
Action:
- Re-derived Proposition 5.1.

Result:
- Given T>=0, D_l>=0, and sum_l D_l + R_res=T:

    R_(l+1)=R_l-D_(l+1)<=R_l;

STEP 021 — Support stochasticity audit, pages 9-12
Action:

Result:

- Equation (35) then adds independent Gumbel noise before Top-k.
- The training target s_i* is a deterministic binary code and no likelihood for the combined

QA disposition:
- BLOCKER B3 confirmed.

Recommended alternatives:
A. Graph support logits + Gumbel-Top-k:

B. Stochastic priority flow + deterministic Top-k:
   - dequantize target priorities;
   - let the flow provide the only support randomness;
   - use deterministic Top-k at generation;
   - do not add independent Gumbel noise.

STEP 031 — Minimal gate proposal
Action:
- Designed gates that separate software validity from empirical superiority.

Required structural gates:
- zero NaN/Inf/negative outputs in at least 10^6 generated events;
- invalid-channel energy exactly zero under defined numerical convention;
- support count equals requested count in every layer/event;
- accounting residual below declared absolute and relative tolerances;
- deterministic replay for fixed seed and controlled diversity across seeds;
- no split-manifest overlap;
- condition permutation test detects dependence.

Required statistical decision rules:
- name a small primary metric set before training;
- define equivalence/non-inferiority margins against the best baseline;
- report confidence intervals across independent training seeds;
- lock the test set and analysis code;
- classify results as pass, fail, or inconclusive.

QA disposition:
- These gates are necessary for a falsifiable protocol.
- Numerical margins must come from detector/reconstruction requirements or a pilot, not be
  invented in the final test analysis.

BLOCKER B1 — Thresholded-readout accounting contradiction
Location:
- Section 3.4, Equations (10)-(11), page 6.
- Section 6, Equation (25), page 9.
- Section 8.2, Equation (37), page 11.
- Corollary 8.2, Equation (39), page 11.
- Canonical tensors and pseudocode, pages 13 and 19-20.

Why it matters:
- The written model cannot represent a layer containing both resolved and subthreshold energy.
- Counts can be declared feasible using energy that should not be allocated to resolved cells.
- The claimed event identity does not match the truth definition.

Required correction:
- Freeze raw-only, thresholded-only, or joint mode.
- In joint mode, define B_plus=D_raw-U before count feasibility and decoding.
- Redefine first-visible and active-layer variables consistently.

Acceptance test:
- Construct synthetic layers with:
  1. only zeros;
  2. only subthreshold hits;
  3. one resolved plus subthreshold hits;
  4. multiple resolved hits plus subthreshold hits;
  5. B_plus=K tau;
  6. B_plus just below and just above K tau.
- Verify exact target reconstruction and accounting under the declared numerical tolerance.

BLOCKER B2 — Untrained or ignored shared latent
Location:
- Section 4, Equation (12), page 7.
- All stage models and objective, pages 7-13.

Why it matters:
- Independent random z with no posterior or invertible training path is not identified by the
  component losses.
- The stated event-level dependence mechanism is therefore unsupported.

Required correction:
- Remove z, or add a complete latent-variable training formulation.
- If using an ELBO, define q_phi, prior, posterior inputs, KL term, discrete auxiliary treatment,
  and generation procedure.
- Add latent-collapse diagnostics if retained.

Acceptance test:
- Compare output distributions under z permutation and z resampling at fixed p4.
- Measure mutual-information proxies or output sensitivity.
- Confirm that removing z changes validated joint dependence if z is claimed to matter.

BLOCKER B3 — Double stochastic support mechanism
Location:
- Continuous state and CFM, pages 9-10.
- Gumbel-Top-k decoder, page 11.
- Support training, page 12.

Why it matters:
- Two independent noise sources govern support without a joint calibrated objective.
- The support endpoint is singular and the training target does not define the generated subset
  distribution.

Required correction:
- Use either deterministic support logits plus Gumbel-Top-k, or stochastic flow priorities plus
  deterministic Top-k.
- Define dequantization if a continuous flow models support.

Acceptance test:
- At fixed c,D,K,G, compare requested and realized inclusion probabilities to held-out truth.
- Verify count exactly K.
- Report subset calibration, per-cell inclusion calibration, pairwise inclusion, and morphology.

BLOCKER B4 — No falsifiable primary decision rule
Location:
- Equation (1), pages 4 and 14-18.
- Stage sequence explicitly labels reports as non-gates.

Why it matters:
- A broad metric menu permits post hoc success claims.
- “Approximate” and “useful” are undefined.

Required correction:
- Predeclare:
  - primary fidelity metrics;
  - primary speed metric;
  - margins or target requirements;
  - confidence level;
  - number of training seeds;
  - test sample size;
  - decision rule for pass/fail/inconclusive;
  - multiplicity handling for secondary metrics.

Acceptance test:
- A blinded analyst given only locked outputs and the analysis script must reach the same verdict.

BLOCKER B5 — Kinetic versus total energy conflation
Location:
- Sections 1.1 and 3.1; Equations (3)-(5); response fraction; pseudocode.

Why it matters:
- Mass-shell validation and Geant4 generator energy can refer to different quantities.
- T/E support and reported energy ranges become ambiguous.

Required correction:
- Use K_inc for kinetic energy and E_tot=K_inc+m_n.
- Record source branches and units.
- Define whether response fraction uses K_inc or E_tot and justify it from the scoring contract.
- Validate momentum using E_tot.

Acceptance test:
- For every event, reconstruct E_tot from p and m_n and compare to the stored total-energy branch.
- Compare K_inc to the generator setting.
- Fail conversion on unit or convention mismatch.

BLOCKER B6 — Reserve non-identifiability
Location:
- Equations (12), (21)-(23), (39); tensor interface; pseudocode.

Why it matters:
- Softmax forces positive reserve.
- No truth target identifies it.
- It can absorb errors and alter the meaning of T.

Required correction:
- Remove reserve if T is total modeled response.
- Otherwise define an observed reserve/leakage target and a zero-inflated model.

Acceptance test:
- Show the exact branch/derivation that supervises reserve.
- Demonstrate closure against truth.
- Report reserve calibration and zero mass.

================================================================
FINAL AUDIT CONCLUSION
================================================================

The central idea should be preserved:
- model visible response explicitly;
- model non-monotone longitudinal deposits through a conserved remaining budget;
- generate finite counts and exact support;
- allocate selected-cell energy with an anti-dust decoder;
- compare hierarchy, graph structure, and parallel flow against competent matched baselines.

The current PDF nevertheless contains mutually incompatible threshold accounting, an
unidentified reserve, an untrained shared latent, a double-random support mechanism, an
ambiguous energy convention, and no numerical decision rule. These are not cosmetic defects.
They affect the mathematical target, training distribution, generated support, and interpretation
of every eventual result.

The correct status is:

“Strong research design with a valid core decoder theorem, but not yet a frozen implementation
contract. Stage 0 audit work and disposable prototypes may proceed; full training and any
performance claim should wait for a revised specification closing B1-B6.”
